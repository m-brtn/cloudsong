# Windgarden artwork

Original assets generated for Cloudsong in the user's Hyper3D account on 2026-11-09. Use follows the [Hyper3D output terms recorded in the project credits](../../CREDITS.md); no exclusive copyright is asserted. No third-party model packs were used.

The meadow creature reference (`meadow-creature-reference.png`) was generated with OpenAI image generation and approved by the user. It was sent to Hyper3D to generate the original mesh. Other accepted assets below were generated from text. The bird remains the original rig expressly selected by the user; later foot experiments are not used.
