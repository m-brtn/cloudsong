# Devlog — 2026-11-08 (Sunday)

## Goal of the day

The first demo game on Quench, made entirely by agents: a calm exploration of floating grassy isles at the golden
hour above a sea of clouds. A small hooded figure with a flowing cape walks, runs, jumps, glides and flies, gathers
drifting lights, lights candles, wakes spirits and brings them to a temple of light. One beautiful region, 10 to 15
minutes of play, on the Mac and the iPhone. Everything procedural (no third-party art).

Afternoon (asked at 14:17): light painting with the engine's new particles and trails (engine stage 14): a ribbon
of light behind the cape while flying or gliding, streams of light looping through the sky near the isles and the
temple, a twinkling field over the meadow, denser as the sun goes down; fewer of each at Light.

## Problems and solutions

- **The clouds cannot be below y = 0.** The engine's weather clamps `cloudBase` to 0 and above. The isles float at
  y = 2200 m instead, above cloud bases at 620 m (cumulus about 1.5 km thick).
- **The sun dimmed and no clouds below the horizon.** The engine dimmed the sun by the cloud cover within 6 km of
  the camera, clouds below it included (0.7: the sun at about 30%), and its sky pass drew the clouds only above the
  horizon. Fixed in the engine (`Weather::coverAbove`; the clouds composited below the horizon over the cloud base;
  the 2D layer drawn from above): commit `4a22a0f`.
- **The fog hid the clouds.** The height fog's base 120 m under the isles, falloff 0.006/m: the air below them was
  opaque. Base at the isles, density 0.00035, falloff 0.0009.
- **The player slid off the hill.** Its slope reached about 46 degrees (Jolt's virtual character slides above 52 with
  the rolling noise added). The hill is wider and lower (radius 16 m, 7 m).
- **No numpy on the machine.** The sounds will be synthesized in C++ at run time instead of Python at build time.
- **The cape stood up over the head in a glide.** Logged numbers (`cape_up`, `cape_back` and a debug print of the
  forces) showed the hem 0.24 m above the collar: the glide sank at 4.3 m/s (gravity 15.5 against a 0.2 s approach
  to 1.45 m/s), so the air from below lifted the cape; and the cloth's damping per frame (0.985) acted as a strong
  drag at 120 frames per second. The glide now scales gravity by 0.1 (sink about 1.5 m/s), the damping works on the
  swing relative to the collar with a rate per second; the hem now trails 0.17 m below the collar, 0.5 m back.
- **The player could not climb the stair, and every slope slowed it down.** On the ground the vertical speed was
  kept at `min(v.y, 0)`: the fall speed built up while grounded and the contact with an upward slope turned it into
  a loss of speed (2.4 m/s of 4.6 up a 20-degree ramp); the stair's risers also caught the capsule. The ground speed
  is now horizontal only, and the stair's collision is a smooth ramp through the middles of the steps (the steps
  are drawn, not collided). On the stair 3.75 m/s forward and 1.3 m up in a second.
- **A debug assertion in Jolt (`AABBTreeToBuffer: Bounding box became smaller`).** The cave's crown ring had NaN
  positions: `pow(cos(pi / 2), 0.7)` with the float cosine slightly below zero. Clamped to zero; static meshes are
  also built around their middle (Jolt keeps a mesh's node bounds as half floats, coarse 2 km up) and skip
  degenerate triangles.
- **`quench_weather_sounds()` failed in the game's build** (`'/assets/make_weather_sounds.py' ... missing`): the
  engine read the script's directory from a variable of its own CMake scope, which a game does not see. Fixed in the
  engine (`56d0b8f`, a cache variable; engine devlog, 05:20); the game pins it.
- **The updraft held the player at about 3 m.** Its lift approached 9 m/s with a time constant of 0.6 s against the
  full gravity: they balanced at -0.36 m/s. The lift now carries the weight (gravity cancelled inside it) and pulls
  toward the column's middle.
- **The player could not climb the temple's tiers** (0.4 m steps; Jolt's stair walking did not lift the capsule over
  them, as on the stair). The tiers collide as a cone over their edges (23 degrees).
- **Space on the title did nothing.** With ImGui's keyboard navigation on, ImGui took the keyboard while the title's
  window was focused, and the engine's key bindings then read as up. Keyboard navigation is off (gamepad navigation
  stays on); Return and Space begin, Escape rests and resumes.
- **The temple's tiers showed grass on top.** `render::cylinderMesh` is open at both ends: the tops were holes onto
  the ground below. The tiers are lathed closed drums with a narrow bevel.
- **The closing card over the title.** `views.txt` went to the title after the end, and the card was drawn over every
  screen. It is drawn only while playing; the title is captured before the end.
- **`tools/package.sh --ios` looked for `Cloudsong.xcodeproj`.** The Xcode generator names the project after the
  CMake project (`cloudsong`), and the app after the target. Fixed; the script also passes an empty bundle id suffix
  (a paid team).
- **A fringe of tufts floating in the air** in the first captures of the rock: the cameras stood inside the meadow's
  rock. Cameras moved outside; the tufts made irregular (in clumps) and darker.
- **The cave's specks looked like stretched teal leaves.** They were cells of the xz plane, drawn out on the walls.
  Cells in 3D, smaller and fewer.
- **The openings in the cave were jagged** (the shell's triangles cut by the holes). Rims of stone round the mouth
  and each hole.
- **The cave's walls an even teal** after the moss covered nearly all of them (the golden image's contrast lost).
  Moss in wide patches with paler stone between them.
- **`line` is a reserved word in HLSL** (glslang): a variable renamed.
- **The engine's first cloud shadow plane changed the engine demo's snow capture** (`demo_snow_sky`, mean 0.1064,
  0.28 % of the pixels over 16): it also moved the map's centre along the sun. The map stays centred under the
  camera; with the default plane the engine's images are the same as before (engine devlog).
- **The stair stays a ramp for the physics.** Its 76 steps rise about 0.7 m each (from y 6 to 60), above the virtual
  character's 0.42 m step-up: not a quick fix.
- **Costs** (numbers in Results): the meadow's GPU time grew about 4% at Beautiful. A/B runs with each new piece
  left out (Balanced, 1920x1080): the rock's bumps 0.15 ms in the isles' view, the hanging tufts 0.05 ms, the motes
  and the beam nothing measurable; the rest is the closer camera and the flowers (`28c86e2`). Skipping triplanar
  planes of a negligible weight saved nothing measurable and was taken out again.
- **The distant "ground" (the sea of clouds) shook while the player stood still** (the user's report, 13:54). Not the
  camera, the physics, the figure or the loop's interpolation: none of them moved between frames. Cause: the
  engine's view rays lost about 2 mrad of precision 2.2 km from the origin (a point on the near plane minus the
  camera's position, in floats), differently in every TAA jitter frame, which moved the clouds below the horizon at
  their grazing angles; the volumetric clouds' accumulation multiplied it. Fixed in the engine (`a66401a`), for every
  game; the pin moved.
- **The streams' ribbons read as white wires in daylight.** Cause: additive light at several times the sky's
  brightness goes white through the tone curve against the bright golden-hour sky (the engine demo's are at the
  blue hour). Solution: saturated colors with peaks below 2, a faint wide halo round each ribbon.
- **The cape trailed while falling.** Cause: "flying" was any time in the air over 0.35 s, so the edge view's fall
  after its teleport drew two vertical lines. Solution: gliding (above 0.2) or a flap only, held 0.3 s.
- **Soft discs near the camera.** Cause: the meadow's points were placed from the camera's own position outward.
  Solution: a ring from 5 m, and a near fade.
- **`kPi` ambiguous** between the game's `Util.h` and a local constant: the local one removed.
- **Costs measured during the engine's gate were noise** (its iOS Simulator tests used the GPU): measured again on
  an idle machine, before and after alternating (Results).

## Limitations and open questions

- The engine's cloud shadow map lies on the plane y = 0 (`docs/weather.md`): cloud shadows are off here. (Since
  07:16 on, on the isles' plane: engine `0a1ace9`.)
- The cave's outside is still a smooth mossy dome (the boulders are gone). The isles' rims show as flat bands.
  (Since the polish round: a lumpier dome with rims at its openings; the rims have tufts and ledges below them.)
- The touch controls ran only under scripted fingers on the Mac; nobody has used them on a phone.
- Nobody has listened to the score or the sounds: they are checked by measurement only.
- The spirits fly in straight lines (through rock if it is in the way); the stair and the temple's tiers collide as
  ramps, so feet float or sink a little on the steps. (Since `28c86e2` the spirits climb over rock ahead of them;
  the stair and the tiers are still ramps.)
- The polish round was judged in still captures only; nobody has seen the capes, the motes, the creatures of light
  or the beam's flare in motion. The cave's moss and the rock's ledges are noise, not modelled shapes; the vines
  under the isles are thin strips, and a few hang in front of the rock as straight lines from some angles.
- The cloud shadow map has one plane (the isles' base height); the isles range over a few tens of metres around it,
  so the error is small, but a cloud rising between two isles of very different heights would shade them slightly
  off. No cloud rises above the isles in this weather, so the shadows change nothing visible yet.
- The new pieces cost GPU time (Results): about +0.4 ms on the meadow and the isles at Balanced on the M4 Pro; on a
  phone at about three times that, an estimate.
- The macOS builds use the local engine override; the iOS build used the pinned commit alone.
- Nothing ran on a phone. With the engine's measured ratio (an iPhone 17 about three times an M4 Pro's GPU time),
  Balanced at the phone's scale would take about 8 to 16 ms on the GPU, the meadow at the edge of 60 Hz: an estimate,
  not a measurement. The thermal governor lowers the render scale and the clouds when the phone grows hot.
- The offscreen times are not frame rates on a display; no on-screen benchmark was run (no windows on the screen
  in this work).
- The shaking's fix was measured offscreen on the Mac; nobody has looked at it on the screen or on a phone yet.
  The camera's smoothing still moves in float steps of 0.24 to 1 mm for about a quarter of a second after a
  teleport or a landing (2.2 km up; harmless since the fix: those frames change as little as the others). The
  engine renders in world coordinates: near the camera positions keep 0.24 mm at this height, enough here.
- The particles and trails were judged in still captures only: nobody has seen the ribbons, the streams or the
  twinkling in motion, on the screen or on a phone. Their costs are offscreen on the Mac; the phone's are not
  measured (the iPhone 17 has the package installed, not launched). The game's sun stands at 10 degrees, so the
  "denser at dusk" factor is fixed at 0.65 in play (it follows `mood sun`). The streams light nothing and cast
  nothing (particles are not in the engine's ray tracing structures and are not light sources).
