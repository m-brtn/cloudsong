## Garment stability and idle-loop repair

The player found continuous cape/hood vibration in the repaired build. The garment solver differentiated float
positions at the world's 2200 m altitude, so a single 0.244 mm rounding step could feed about 0.029 m/s back into
velocity at 120 Hz. Variable final substeps and an always-on flutter force added further motion at rest.

The solver now uses world-oriented coordinates relative to the hero's nearby origin, a fixed 120 Hz accumulator,
and interpolated driving poses/colliders. Render poses interpolate in Chest/Head space and attach to the current
body, keeping sewn edges aligned even on frames without a simulation tick. Wind is filtered; synthetic flutter
only accompanies movement/flight. Increased damping retains inertia while allowing the garments to settle.
World-foot interpolation also uses `previous + (current - previous) * alpha`: GLM's weighted sum could change an
identical pair of 2200 m endpoints by one float step, introducing artificial movement while standing still.

A complete idle-loop check then exposed one remaining impulse at 8.8083 s: the cape's target changed by about
0.58 mm in one frame despite a stationary root and 15 cm clearance from the nearest collider. The original
preparation closed the idle clip over only its final two keys. Its new Hermite bridge spans 0.4 s on either side
of the wrap, matching the surrounding pose and tangent. The original motion export remains untouched; every
Jogging and Walking track and timestamp was compared and remains bit-identical. Idle hip translation's sampled
seam velocity discontinuity fell from 0.003376 to 0.000110 m/s; the chest rotation discontinuity fell from
0.001197 to 0.0000547 rad/s. No garment mesh or skin weights changed in this repair.

Verification:

- An isolated CPU harness ran the exact garment solver and real rig metadata with a constant body/breeze for
  20 seconds at 30, 60, 120 and 144 updates/s, at both 0 m and 2200 m altitude. Both altitudes produced identical
  results; the maximum final cape-pose difference between update rates was 4.34 micrometres. Settled cape jitter
  was at most 0.0000237 m/s and hood jitter 0.000000078 m/s. Move/stop checks retained inertia and settled again.
  This isolates the solver; it does not exercise the game renderer or recorded body animation.
- Actual-game offscreen checks used both deterministic fixed-time frames and uncapped real frame time. The real
  run averaged about 190 frames/s; requesting 30/60/120 limits did not cap this offscreen mode, so those are not
  claimed as actual-game frame-rate checks. Its idle peaks before the loop repair were cape 0.0192 and hood
  0.0412 m/s. Thirty before/after idle captures were inspected: the old crown changed direction almost every
  frame; the new crown drifts smoothly and the cape remains calm.
- The persistent `garments` scenario passed **19/19 assertions** after the loop repair, including a full nine-second
  idle observation, locomotion, stop/settle and glide. The first full-loop run had failed one assertion with cape
