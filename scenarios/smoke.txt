# Smoke check (ctest `smoke`): the game starts on the meadow, the keyboard moves the player and a jump leaves the
# ground, and the app quits cleanly. App time advances 1/120 s per frame in scripted runs.
#   cloudsong --script scenarios/smoke.txt

wait 0.5
expect grounded == 1

# W runs away from the camera (+z at the start).
mark z
down W
wait 0.5
up W
expect d_z > 1.2

mark y
tap Space
wait 0.2
expect d_y > 0.5
wait 1.0
expect grounded == 1
quit
